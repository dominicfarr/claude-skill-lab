Source: #4. Users of a read-it-later app (mobile and desktop) tag saved articles with freeform labels and filter their saved list by tag.

Assumptions (no one to ask): single user per account, tags are per-user, saving and listing articles already exist, tags are case-insensitive, filter is by one tag first.
