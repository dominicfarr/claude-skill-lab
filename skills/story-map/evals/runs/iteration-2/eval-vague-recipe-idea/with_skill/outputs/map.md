## Recipe management tool

A personal tool for a home cook to collect, find, cook from and plan with their own recipes. Assumed: single user, no sharing, web app.

Legend: 🦴 = in the walking-skeleton slice (has BDD specs)

### Collect recipes

**Add a recipe**
- 🦴 Type in a recipe by hand
- Import a recipe from a web page link
- Add a photo of the dish
- Paste in recipe text and have it split into ingredients and method

**Edit or remove a recipe**
- Edit a saved recipe
- Delete a recipe
- Duplicate a recipe to make a variation

### Find a recipe

**Browse my collection**
- 🦴 See all my recipes in a list
- Mark a recipe as favourite
- Organise recipes into collections or tags

**Search and filter**
- 🦴 Search recipes by title
- Search by ingredient I have on hand
- Filter by cooking time or dietary need

### Cook a recipe

**Follow the recipe**
- 🦴 Open a recipe to read ingredients and method
- View a recipe in cooking mode (big text, one step at a time)
- Scale servings up or down
- Start a timer from a step

**Capture what I learned**
- Rate a recipe
- Add a personal note after cooking

### Plan and shop

**Plan meals**
- Add a recipe to a day in a weekly plan

**Build a shopping list**
- 🦴 Generate a shopping list from selected recipes
- Tick items off while shopping
- Exclude pantry items from the list
